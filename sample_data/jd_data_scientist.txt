Job Title: Data Scientist

We are seeking a Data Scientist to join our analytics team. The ideal
candidate has strong experience in Python, Machine Learning, and Natural
Language Processing (NLP). Hands-on experience with Pandas and NumPy for
data analysis is required. Familiarity with TensorFlow or PyTorch for
building predictive models is a plus. 3+ years of experience required.

Responsibilities:
- Build and evaluate machine learning models
- Perform data analysis using Python, Pandas, and NumPy
- Communicate findings to stakeholders
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams in an agile environment
